專案名稱

l earn -- web3 MOOC

專案簡介

We are developing an online learning platform based on Web3 technology. Our platform aims to address the limitations inherent in Web2 platforms and improve real-world education. We will use NFTs, AI, and token economics to create an environment where all participants, including course providers and active contributors, have sufficient incentives to provide a more engaging and effective learning experience. Importantly, this system will not only benefit online learners and educators, but also real-world education improvement through DAO to allocate funds in our token economics.

為什麼要做這個專案？（動機）

Token economics are changing our world economic system and providing a way for people to increase their income. The "Play to Earn" model, popularized by games like Axie Infinity, has shown that people are willing to spend time and effort to earn tokens that can be used in real world. Inspired by this, we are designing a 'Learn to Earn' platform, which aims to incentivize learning and improve education in the real world.

請描述此專案預計解決的問題？

1. Lack of interaction (ex. Discussion, feedback)
2. Low completion rates
3. Credentialing issues

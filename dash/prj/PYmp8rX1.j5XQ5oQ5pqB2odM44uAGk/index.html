專案簡介

在我們的平台上會根據捐款將要捐獻給的組織類型來創建 NFT 池。希望參與捐款過程的藝術家將能夠直接在我們的平台上將他們的作品鑄成 NFT，並選擇一個 NFT 池提交。加密貨幣的擁有者將被提示選擇他們希望質押的 NFT 池。智能合約會使用他們借出的資產進行 Yield Farming，並將收益捐贈給與該池對應的慈善組織同時獲得 NFT 作為憑證與獎勵。捐助者的資本本金將不會有任何損失，並且可以在任何時候提取他們的資產。

徐子修：台大資工，Amber Group 區塊鏈安全研究員，AppWorks School 區塊鏈導師，專案中擔任工程師

為什麼要做這個專案？（動機）

Cryptocurrencies and NFTs have long been criticized for being nothing more than investment tools and medium for money laundering/fraud. Our team deeply believes that digital assets can be used for much greater purposes. That is why we came up with the project, in hopes to build a community where crypto and NFTs are used to do good.

請描述此專案預計解決的問題？

The ultimate motivation behind this idea is to simplify the way for everyone to engage in philanthropy. One could contribute directly just by staking their crypto assets on our platform and following a few straightforward steps. Besides, there are different types of NFT pools and charity organizations provided for decisions.
